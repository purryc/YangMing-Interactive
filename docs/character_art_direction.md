# 王阳明国漫美术设定

使用原创历史角色演绎。水墨参考控制笔触和留白，角色板参考控制信息布局。全龄锚点是略不对称的直眉、狭长眼、窄鼻梁和左眼下一颗淡痣。幼年脸颊更圆，中年骨相收紧，晚年加深纹路与花白发须。相近年龄沿用前一张脸，不因更衣而换五官。淡痣是美术设定。

## 统一提示词

```text
Create a 16:9 character identity board for Wang Yangming, an original Ming-era historical manhua interpretation. Hand-painted Chinese ink wash and restrained mineral pigment on off-white xuan paper. Preserve the supplied stage's same facial identity across every view. Long almond eyes, subtly asymmetric straight brows, narrow high nose bridge, small faint mole under the left eye, natural proportions and a quiet observant gaze. Age naturally. Show a large full-body pose, matching neutral front, back and side views, one attitude pose, four distinct expressions, outfit and key prop details, silhouette and a small palette. Clean asymmetric layout, generous blank space, separated figures, unobstructed faces and limbs. No landscape, no logos, no modern clothing, no fantasy armor, no dense text, no photorealism, no 3D.
```
